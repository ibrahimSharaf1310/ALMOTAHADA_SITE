ALMOTAHDA CONTRACTING

Al Motahada for Contracting & Engineering works is a leading general contractor in Egypt, with over four decades of proven excellence in the construction industry. Classified as a Class A contractor, we deliver high-quality, innovative projects across diverse sectors including Commercial, Industrial, Educational, Healthcare, Residential & Resorts, as well as Infrastructure and Roads. Our reputation is built on a steadfast commitment to efficiency, quality, and innovation, supported by strong work ethics and a deep dedication to health, safety, and environmental standards. Over the years, we have successfully executed landmark projects that reflect our ability to combine technical expertise with practical solutions. Beyond Egypt, we proudly extend our services to international markets, continuing to showcase our capability to meet the highest global standards in contracting and engineering.
